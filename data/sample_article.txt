Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence by machines, especially computer systems. It includes learning through data (machine learning), reasoning with logic, and perceiving its environment. Modern AI systems learn from large datasets, identify patterns, and make decisions or predictions with minimal human intervention. Applications range from recommendation engines and voice assistants to self-driving cars and medical diagnostics. Training AI models requires substantial computing power and quality data, while responsible AI emphasizes fairness, transparency, accountability, and safety. As AI becomes more capable, understanding its strengths, limitations, and ethical implications is increasingly important for everyone.
