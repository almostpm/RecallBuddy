How RecallBuddy Supercharges Learning

Cognitive science principles built right into a clean monochrome digital interface.

1. Active Retrieval Loop

COGNITIVE

Standard reading is passive—it is easy for your eyes to glide over text without your brain absorbing it. RecallBuddy stops you and asks, "Based on what you just read, what is the answer to this?" This active testing forces your brain to store the information permanently.

2. Zero Time Wasted

EFFICIENCY

It identifies exactly what you don't know. Instead of re-reading an entire 50-page chapter, you can instantly see the specific concept parts you got wrong and focus your energy only on fixing those exact gaps.

3. On-The-Spot Explanations

FEEDBACK

When you get a question wrong, it doesn't just display a plain "Incorrect." It immediately explains why you were wrong in plain, digestible language so you fix the misunderstanding right away.

4. Brain Fitness Tracker

ANALYTICS

It keeps tabs on how well you are doing over time. Acting like a fitness tracker for your brain, it displays your accuracy rate, total retention score, and mastery level across subjects.
